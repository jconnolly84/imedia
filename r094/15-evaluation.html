Final Evaluation (Written Review)

Evaluate how successful your final product is.

Keep your Project Log updated Screenshot as you work Link back to brief + audience

Evaluation

  • Compare against: client brief, target audience, and your pre‑production (mind map/mood board/concepts/visualisation).
  • State strengths (what meets requirements well).
  • State weaknesses (what could be improved).
  • Suggest specific improvements and explain why they would help.
Project Log evidence:
Add screenshots and explain what you did and why.

Quick checks

  • Does this link back to the client brief?
  • Does this suit the target audience?
  • Have you taken screenshots during the process?
  • Is your work saved in a sensible, organised folder?
Common mistake:
Only showing the final outcome. Record your process and explain your decisions as you go.

Looking Back Critically

Honest review

A good review compares the product against the client's requirements and audience, using specific examples rather than general praise.

Key Terms

Strength
A part that works well, with a reason.
Weakness
A part that works less well, with a reason.
Improvement
A specific, realistic change and its benefit.

Good Practice & Watch Out For

✔ Good practice

  • Use specific examples from the product
  • Link points to the client and audience
  • Suggest realistic improvements

✘ Watch out for

  • Only describing what you did
  • Vague praise like 'it looks good'
  • Improvements with no reason
