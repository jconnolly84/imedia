Concept Sketches

Develop a range of meaningfully different concepts for a visual identity.

Keep your Project Log updated Screenshot as you work Link back to brief + audience

Concept Sketches

  • Each concept must be meaningfully different (not tiny tweaks).
  • Label each concept clearly and annotate it.
  • Screenshot/scan/photo the concepts and insert into the Project Log.
Project Log evidence:
Add screenshots and explain what you did and why.

Quick checks

  • Does this link back to the client brief?
  • Does this suit the target audience?
  • Have you taken screenshots during the process?
  • Is your work saved in a sensible, organised folder?
Common mistake:
Only showing the final outcome. Record your process and explain your decisions as you go.

Key Terms

Concept sketch
A quick drawing exploring a design idea.
Logo
A graphic mark representing a brand.
Strapline
A short slogan used with a brand.

Good Practice & Watch Out For

✔ Good practice

  • Make ideas genuinely different
  • Label colours, fonts and symbols
  • Consider how each idea suits the brand

✘ Watch out for

  • Small variations of one idea
  • Overly complex logos that won't scale
  • Ignoring the audience
