Reading Between the Lines

What the client needs

A brief describes the business, its values, its audience and the products needed. Picking these out tells you what the visual identity must communicate.

Business type

What they do

  • e.g. café, charity, tech start-up
Brand values

What they stand for

  • e.g. eco-friendly, playful, trustworthy
Positioning

Economy · mid-range · high-end

  • Affects colour, type and complexity

Key Terms

Visual identity
The visual elements that represent a brand: name, logo, slogan, colours, typography.
Brand values
What a brand believes in and wants to be known for.
Brand positioning
Where a brand sits in the market: economy, mid-range or high-end.

Good Practice & Watch Out For

✔ Good practice

  • Identify the business type, values and positioning
  • Note who the audience is and what appeals to them
  • Note constraints such as sizes and formats

✘ Watch out for

  • Starting to design before understanding the brand
  • Ignoring brand positioning
  • Assuming your own taste matches the audience's
