Task 1A(iv) Explain your ideas

Explain how your ideas meet the brief and appeal to the audience.

Explain how ideas meet the brief Explain appeal to audience Be specific
Don't waffle:
Avoid vague claims like "it looks modern". Describe what makes it modern (spacing, typography, colour palette, icon style).

What to write (short but detailed)

  • List each **client requirement** and explain how your design meets it.
  • Explain how style choices appeal to the audience (colour, tone, imagery).
  • Explain why your features improve usability (navigation, buttons, layout).
  • Mention constraints (time, software, skills) and how you adapted.

Sentence starters

  • The brief requires… so I will…
  • This appeals to the audience because…
  • I chose this layout so users can…
  • I rejected idea B because… (then justify).
