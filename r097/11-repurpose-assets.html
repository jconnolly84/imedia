Repurposing evidence

  • Annotated screenshots - Before/after comparisons (original vs repurposed).
  • What changed: crop, resize, colour correction, compression, format conversion.
  • Why it changed: faster load, better readability, consistency, suitability for web.
  • Export settings (quality %, pixel size).

Repurposing examples

  • Cropping a photo to a consistent aspect ratio for cards.
  • Converting PNG photos to JPG/WebP to reduce file size.
  • Resizing large images down to the actual on-page size.
  • Creating a transparent PNG icon from a larger graphic.
