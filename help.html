Help

What's on the site, where to find it, and quick fixes for the problems that come up most often.

Finding your way around

  • R093 topic guides: one page for each of the 36 specification topics, with flashcards and, for most topics, a podcast and a short video.
  • Revision games: quick recall games, grouped by topic.
  • Exam practice: mini exams, the June 2025 paper and the 9-mark tools for extended answers.
  • R094 and R097: step-by-step guides for the graphics and website coursework units, plus a Dreamweaver build guide.
  • iMedia Tools: PhotoCraft, VectorCraft and Voice Studio, for making assets in the browser.

Something isn't working

  • The page looks out of date after an update. Force a refresh with Ctrl + F5, or Cmd + Shift + R on a Mac.
  • Videos are blank. School networks often block YouTube. Try at home, or ask your IT team to allow youtube-nocookie.com.
  • A game freezes or its buttons do nothing. Try Chrome or Edge, and turn off any extension that blocks scripts.
  • The leaderboard says to launch from the worksheet app. Class leaderboards are only for the site author's own classes. The game works exactly the same without one.

Where's my progress gone?

Progress is saved in the browser you used, on that device. It won't show up on a different computer, in a private or incognito window, or after your browser data has been cleared. School computers that wipe themselves when you log off will lose it too.

Using it with a class

Topic guides work well on the board as lesson starters. The games are good for retrieval practice, and the mini exams and the June 2025 paper give timed practice. Everything is free to use in lessons and for homework, with no sign-up. Ask students to skip the name and Submit boxes on the exam papers, because submissions only go to the site author's own classes.
