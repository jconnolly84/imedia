Photo editing / Raster graphics

PhotoCraft

A layer-based photo editor that works like the professional tools. Retouch photos, combine images and build adverts, posters and website graphics.

  • AI background removal, object removal and 2× resolution boost, all run on your device
  • Layers, masks and blending, with ready-made canvas sizes for A4, presentations and more
  • The school font library: 148 font families, sorted by category
  • Automatic recovery copies about once a minute, in case your tab crashes

Vector design / Logos & icons

VectorCraft

Vector illustration for crisp logos, icons and layouts that stay sharp at any size, with a student-friendly first view so it isn't overwhelming.

  • 20 everyday tools: pen, shapes, shape builder, type, gradient and more
  • Properties and layers ready on screen; every other panel is one click away in the Window menu
  • The same 148-family school font library, plus fonts from your own computer
  • Recovery copies every minute, kept safely in your browser

Audio / Voice-overs & narration

Voice Studio

Type or paste your script, pick a British English voice and download a voice-over ready for Audacity, Canva or your video editor.

  • Four main British voices, extra voices, and a mixer to blend two into your own
  • Download as WAV or MP3, with a preview for every voice
  • Speech is generated on your own device: your script never leaves the browser
  • Scripts up to 5,000 characters (about five minutes of speech)

Good to know

Your work lives in
your browser.

Nothing you make is uploaded to a server. That keeps it private, but it also means there's no cloud copy: download your work before you close the tab.

Save little and often, with clear file names, into your project folder.
  1. Use a laptop or desktop. Chrome or Edge works best. These are full creative tools, so phones aren't recommended.
  2. Click inside the editor first. Some shortcuts (like Ctrl+T or Ctrl+D) also belong to the browser. Click the canvas so the tool gets them, or use the menus.
  3. Voice Studio's first run takes a minute. It downloads its voice model (about 92 MB) once, then remembers it. If it's blocked, ask IT to allow huggingface.co.
  4. Recovery is a safety net, not a save. Recovery copies stay in that browser only and can be cleared by school profile clean-ups.
