Cookie Notice

iMedia Genius doesn't set any cookies of its own. There's no cookie banner because there's nothing to opt out of: no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Browser storage instead of cookies

The site uses local storage. It's similar to a cookie, but it stays in your browser and isn't sent to a server every time a page loads. It saves three kinds of thing:

  • Progress: imediaDoneTopics, imediaRevisionTasksV1 and imediaStudioProgress:… remember which topics and guides you've marked as done, and your revision task list.
  • Your answers: imediagenius_topic-…_exam_practice_v2, imediagenius_pre_mock_01_save and R093-2025-JUN::… keep your answers to topic exam questions, the mini exam and the June 2025 paper while you work on them.
  • Engagement links: imediaEngagementSession and imediaEngagementQueue only appear for students in the site author's own classes who arrive from a teacher's engagement link. Other visitors never get them. See the Privacy Policy.

Cookies from YouTube

Most embedded videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which doesn't set cookies until you press play. A few older pages use the standard player, which may set cookies as soon as the page loads.

Clearing it all

To remove everything the site has saved, clear the site data for imediagenius.co.uk in your browser settings. In Chrome or Edge, click the icon to the left of the address, open Site settings and choose Delete data. This resets your progress and deletes your saved answers.
