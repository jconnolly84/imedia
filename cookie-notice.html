Cookie Notice

iMedia Genius doesn't set any cookies of its own. There's no cookie banner because there's nothing to opt out of: no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Browser storage instead of cookies

The site uses local storage. It's similar to a cookie, but it stays in your browser and isn't sent to a server every time a page loads. These are the only items it saves:

  • imediaStudioProgress:… records which guides you've ticked off on each course.
  • R093-PM01::autosave_v1 and imediagenius_pre_mock_01_save keep your mini exam answers while you're working on them.
  • imediaEngagementSession and imediaEngagementQueue are only created if you arrive from a teacher's engagement link. They hold that session and any activity waiting to be sent. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Cookies from other services

  • YouTube. Most embedded videos use privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which doesn't set cookies until you press play. A few older pages use the standard player, which may set cookies as soon as the page loads.
  • Google sign-in. On the teacher marking desk, Firebase Authentication remembers the teacher's Google sign-in. Students never see this.

Clearing it all

To remove everything the site has saved, clear the site data for imediagenius.co.uk in your browser settings. In Chrome or Edge, click the icon to the left of the address, open Site settings and choose Delete data. This resets your guide progress and deletes any mini exam answers you haven't submitted.
