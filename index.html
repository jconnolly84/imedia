What iMedia Genius does

One place to revise the whole R093 course without guessing what to do next.

iMedia Genius is a game-based revision hub for OCR Creative iMedia. It brings topic guides, videos, podcasts, flashcards, exam practice and retrieval games together so students can move from learning content to applying it under exam conditions.

  • 1 Learn each topic with clear textbook-style guides, worked examples and media-specific vocabulary.
  • 2 Practise through low-stakes games, mini exams, 9-mark tasks and full-paper style questions.
  • 3 Use progress tracking and random revision routes to keep revision varied, active and easy to revisit.

Who it is for

Designed for OCR Creative iMedia students, teachers and anyone revising R093: Creative iMedia in the Media Industry.

Students Find quick revision routes, active recall tasks and exam practice without jumping between platforms.
Teachers Use the topic pages and games for starters, homework, revision lessons and intervention.
