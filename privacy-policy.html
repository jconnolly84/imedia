Privacy Policy

iMedia Genius is a free revision site for OCR Creative iMedia. You don't need an account, there are no adverts, and nothing here tracks you for marketing. This page lists exactly what gets stored or sent, and when.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Using the site normally

Reading the topic guides, playing the revision games and watching the videos needs no sign-in and no personal details. Nothing you do is sent to iMedia Genius unless you open the site from a teacher's engagement link or submit a mini exam. Both are explained below.

What stays in your browser

A few features save to your browser's local storage, so your work is still there next time you use the same device:

  • Guides you tick off as done, so the course progress bar remembers where you got to.
  • Your answers in the markable mini exam, which save as you type so closing the tab doesn't lose them.

This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and it won't follow you to another device or browser.

Teacher engagement links

Some teachers set activities from the iMedia Genius worksheet app. Those links carry a one-off code. When you open one, the site swaps the code for a session of up to eight hours, linked to the name and class your teacher set up. It also removes the code from the address bar.

While that session is active, the site records:

  • which pages you open, and when you switch away or leave
  • how long you are active on each page, checked every 30 seconds
  • how much of each video and podcast you watch or hear
  • your game scores, which appear on your class leaderboard

This goes to iMedia Genius's Firebase servers in London (Google Cloud, europe-west2) so your teacher can see how revision is going. If you visit the site any other way, none of this is recorded.

Mini exams and the marking desk

The markable mini exam asks for your name and class before you submit. Your answers are stored in Firebase so your teacher can mark them on the marking desk. Teachers sign in to the marking desk with a Google account. Students never need one.

Services the site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub keeps server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces, so your browser downloads them from Google's servers.
  • YouTube plays the embedded videos. Most use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Firebase, part of Google, stores engagement sessions, scores and mini exam submissions, as described above.

Links to Pexels, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and NotebookLM take you to other websites. Once you're there, their own privacy policies apply.

For schools

The site is made for Key Stage 4 students taking R093, R094 and R097. If you use engagement links or mini exams, student names and class codes are processed as described on this page. Check that this fits your school's data protection arrangements before using them with a class.

Changes

If the site starts storing or sending anything new, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
